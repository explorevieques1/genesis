# `JM` Marks

Ranges of candles you marked — entries, exits and ideas, with the note you wrote

Opens with `JM` · home page **journal** · [[Connection Graph]]

Marked on the chart: press-drag a zone over the bars in [[Module CH — Chart|CH]],
select it, press **journal**. A saved range from
[[Module CR — Candle Ranges|CR]] opens as the series `CR:<id>` and marks the
same way. Picking a row here reopens the chart on that series.

## Agents

- none — this is the operator's own hand. [[Agent — Insight Miner]] reads what
  it writes.
